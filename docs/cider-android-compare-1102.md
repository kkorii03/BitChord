# Cider for Android (beta, cider.sh/downloads/android) vs BitChord 1.9 — from the user's 5 screenshots (11-02)

Cider Android = Apple Music content and layout, with Material 3 Expressive motion and shapes.

## Now playing
- Full-bleed artwork at the top that dissolves into a blurred, artwork-tinted lower half (no hard edge).
- Title bold + artist grey at the left; favourite (star) and ••• at the right.
- Wavy progress line (M3 Expressive squiggle) for the played part, flat for the rest; elapsed at left, remaining (-1:24) at right, and in the middle "116 BPM · G" — tempo and key of the song. BitChord already analyses BPM/key for Automix: show them the same way when confidence is good.
- Controls: three large rounded pills — previous | wide play/pause | next.
- Bottom sheet handle with "Playing from <source>" (e.g. Discovery Station) + lyrics button + output-device button.

## Tab bar and mini player
- Floating pill tab bar: the selected tab expands into icon + label ("Home", "New", "Radio", "Library"), the others are icons only; search is a tab.
- Mini player floats above it as a rounded card: artwork, title/artist, a thin wavy progress line, a pause pill and next.

- Album: whole page tinted with the artwork's colour; centred artwork; "Title - Single"; artist with a round avatar; "Dance · 2022"; buttons shuffle | wide Play | +; track list in a rounded card (number, title, duration, •••); footer "October 14, 2022 · 1 song · 2 min" and ℗ label line; share button top right.

2. Floating pill tab bar whose selected tab shows its label; search as a tab; mini player as a floating card above it.
3. Now playing: artwork dissolving into a tinted blur; three pill controls; "Playing from …" line; BPM · key in the middle of the time row.
4. Song rows in rounded cards with duration at the right and explicit badge.
5. Section captions over hero cards ("Made for You", "최신 앨범").
