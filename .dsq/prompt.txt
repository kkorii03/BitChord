MEDIUM — TelegramDatabaseKeyStore.kt:21-22 — any failure to read/decrypt the sealed key (transient I/O, invalidated Keystore key) silently calls replace(), generating a new key while the old encrypted db/files remain, making the saved session permanently unreadable. MEDIUM — TelegramClientManager.kt:267-269 and :148-153 — after a 5s/20s close timeout the code starts a new client or deletes db/files while the old native client may still be closing, risking two clients on one db or deleting files under an open client.
