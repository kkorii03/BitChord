Chroma ambiguity is out of scope for this task.
- A same-song comparison shows the app also accepts grossly wrong PROVIDER timings without any correction: v59g4HRNIkc got KuGou (-8.68 s FAIL) in one run and Gemini (-0.02 s PASS) in another; ekzHIouo8Q4 got 벅스 with base=none (-11.85 s FAIL) and PaxSenix with base=ai (+11662 ms, -0.046 s PASS). The selection of provider is not stable across runs.
- So the target is: when the heard transcript clearly disagrees with the accepted timing by more than the narrow refine can move, the app must not settle. Either escalate (wide refine / AI batch) or prefer a provider whose timings agree with the transcript. Choose the smallest one that the code supports.
