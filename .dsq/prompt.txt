HIGH — `shared/src/jvmSharedMain/kotlin/com/music/bitchord/data/YtMusicRepository.kt:1263`
The retry clears `songVersionWaived` before awaiting the still-in-flight lookup, so that lookup can read `waitedOut=false` at line 1387, cache the timed-out miss in `songVersionMissing`, and the retry then returns it without the intended fresh search, freezing the video again.

MEDIUM — `app/src/main/java/com/music/bitchord/playback/SongVersionController.kt:113`
The retry rebuilds the row from the raw MediaItem, losing `lookupSong`'s synthetic `typeUnknown=true`, stripped `localUri`, and stored upload type, so for legacy/unknown downloaded video rows `wantsSongVersion`/`keepsLocalFile` makes the retry return null immediately while still spending the one retry.
Fix: store the exact lookup `Song` (or its verify/stored flags) with the pending retry and pass that to `songVersion` instead of re-deriving it from the current item.
